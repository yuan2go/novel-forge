## Draft

Fulfil binding constraints without explaining the checklist to the reader. Return scene_artifacts only, with no second whole-chapter body or self-assessment.

Let characters respond to what they perceive, want, remember and risk in this situation. Do not manufacture a penalty, reward or reversal for every action.

Use settled progress as the next scene's starting point. Revisit it only when a new perspective, consequence or purpose gives the return meaning. These inputs assist composition and should never appear as a checklist or engineering vocabulary in the prose.
