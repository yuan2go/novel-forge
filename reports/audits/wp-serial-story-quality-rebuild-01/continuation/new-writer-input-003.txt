## Draft

Write each frozen Scene Slot once in its declared order, treating the chapter as one continuous narrative. The Plan fixes outcomes, knowledge and obligations; realize its unspecified dialogue, gesture, imagery and timing through the characters. Fulfil binding constraints without explaining the checklist to the reader. Return scene_artifacts only, with no second whole-chapter body or self-assessment.

Let characters respond to what they perceive, want, remember and risk in this situation. A possible consequence is conditional; binding fictional outcomes retain their force. Use dialogue, hesitation, attention and action where they naturally carry subtext. Do not manufacture a penalty, reward or reversal for every action.

Use settled progress as the next scene's starting point. Revisit it only when a new perspective, consequence or purpose gives the return meaning. A peaceful scene may advance understanding or a relationship without escalating conflict.
