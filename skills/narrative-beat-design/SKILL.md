# Narrative Beat Design

## Shared rules

Use frozen author intent, character motives and approved design facts. Ground the reader's necessary concepts and expectations before later action relies on them. Separate a proposed development from an accepted event. Use only permitted abstract references and return the task's declared schema.

## Theme question

Form a dramatic question that characters can test through competing choices and consequences. Let opposing answers have credible human or social pressures. Connect the question to the supplied premise without dictating a moral lecture or forcing every scene to state the theme. Provide the requested original alternatives and their story implications.

## Beat planning

Order action, perception, information and emotional changes so each beat has usable prerequisites and consequences. A beat can do more than one compatible job; identify its main purpose without forcing artificial segmentation. Place grounding close enough to its use to be remembered, and use subtext or action where explanation would stall the scene. Hook and payoff horizons follow the actual promise and plan, not a fixed chapter count.
