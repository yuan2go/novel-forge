# Writing theory methods

Read only the supplied Source material. Locate each author's claim in that exact edition
and reading range. Separate an explicit author statement, the analyst's interpretation,
and a proposed application. Missing chapters, unread pages and unsupported views remain
unknown. A book title or a remembered teaching supplies no evidence.

Extract a method for a specific writing problem rather than a summary or a list of rules.
Describe its suitable stage, operation, prerequisites, exceptions, misuse and a way to
revise an unsuccessful application. Preserve differing authors' conditions. Strong advice
in a dramatic example does not establish a universal constraint on every story or scene.
Evidence of a source claim does not establish improved prose or reader preference.

For character reactions, distinguish visible conduct from a person's purpose, past,
relationships, perception and available knowledge. The same conduct can express different
purposes. Propose how the relevant basis could affect a choice, attention or response;
do not require a biography or an explanation of every emotion in the prose.

For connected events, distinguish an intended result from what occurs and the conditions
available afterward. An application can carry changed opportunities, resources, knowledge
or relationships into later action. Unexpected opposition, loss, escalation and reversal
remain techniques with conditions, rather than required consequences of every action.

For dialogue, consider what a speaker wants now, whom the speech addresses, the action
attempted through it and the response that changes the next exchange. Vocabulary and
rhythm can reflect experience and the relationship. Direct requests, frank disclosure,
silence and everyday talk can serve a purpose; hidden meaning is not mandatory. Removing
unnecessary explanation must not remove information the reader needs.

Only supports[].support_text may retain the shortest exact source quotation required to
verify a claim, with its actual chunk and UTF-8 byte offsets. These restricted evidence
fields are not reusable knowledge or draft inputs. Use original analysis in all statements
and method fields; never carry these quotations into a new novel.

Return findings in the supplied Observation schema with exact Source evidence and explicit
uncertainty. The downstream abstraction is SOURCE_ABSTRACTION / HYPOTHESIS and requires
its existing MKAOS approval. Preserve actual premises and exceptions without manufacturing
world laws, character facts or hard plot constraints. Macro methods belong at appropriate
design or planning scope; do not force them into a technique quota for each Scene.

Do not copy source examples, characters, settings, speech or distinctive event combinations
into a method for new fiction. Original positive, negative and misuse examples belong in
separate teaching material, marked as teaching only; they are not facts about the Book.
The current Book's planning process decides ADOPT, ADAPT or REJECT against its own inputs.
