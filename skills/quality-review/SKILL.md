# Quality Review

## Shared rules

Judge the supplied candidate against its author intent, accepted facts and binding task constraints. Distinguish a demonstrated conflict from uncertainty and a discretionary aesthetic suggestion. Cite exact words or exact structured input references supporting a finding, explain the effect on the reader, and propose the smallest useful repair. The host binds versions and locations; this judgment is not human approval or Authority acceptance. Return the declared JSON judgment.

## Chapter review

Read the candidate as a narrative. Check whether action follows character motive and knowledge, scenes connect, disclosures are permitted, and the chapter delivers its intended movement. Preserve effective voice, images, subtext and tension. Require a cost, growth, reward, reversal or hook only when the applicable author or obligation contract calls for it. ReReview judges the exact revised text and carried findings without inventing new history.

## Quality dimension

Evaluate only the requested dimension and its frozen criteria. Support each finding with this candidate's evidence; state insufficient evidence when needed. Distinguish a structural/schema failure, a source boundary failure and a literary judgment. Do not replace the requested dimension with a universal checklist or infer audience approval from a score.

## Design evaluation

Evaluate the exact design candidate against its canonical parents and the declared layer profiles. Check semantic support, consistency, creative specificity and executable consequences at this layer. Reference-only fields are lineage, not missing embedded assets. Qualification criteria guide this judgment; report insufficient evidence and bounded repairs rather than inventing PASS.

## Longform review

Review the exact accepted prose window and its declared purpose. Track causal continuity, character development, repeated explanation, voice, promise setup and payoff across the supplied chapters. Attribute each concern to concrete passages and distinguish a deliberate slow development from a missed due obligation. Do not extrapolate unseen chapters or convert a suggestion into accepted story state.

## Screenplay review

Compare the exact screenplay with its supplied edition ranges. Assess causal fidelity, visible dramatic action, speaker knowledge, dialogue, permitted audience disclosures and performability. Identify concrete scenes and beats needing repair, retain effective adaptation choices, and record uncertainty when the source supports more than one interpretation. Use screenplay findings, not novel Slot output rules.
