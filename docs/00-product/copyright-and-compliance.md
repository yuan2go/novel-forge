# Copyright And Compliance

## Boundary

Popular novels and professional books may be used only for structural decomposition, mechanism extraction, and commercial logic analysis.

## Forbidden

- Copying original sentences or distinctive expressions.
- Reusing protected characters, settings, and plot combinations.
- Mixing source text with generated drafts.
- Treating decomposition reports as draft text.

## Required Separation

- Raw source: `data/raw/`.
- Cleaned source: `data/cleaned/`.
- Abstract decomposition: `data/decompositions/`.
- Reusable knowledge/material: `data/knowledge/`, `data/materials/`.
- Generated fiction assets: `data/topics/`, `data/worlds/`, `data/characters/`, `data/outlines/`, `data/chapter_plans/`, `data/drafts/`.
- Review/feedback: `data/reviews/`, `data/feedback/`.

## Acceptance

Reference-derived JSON assets must declare an abstract structure copyright boundary in metadata.
